**Winner of the Best Paper Award at IEEE RFID 2025.**

SenSync tackles key challenges in RFID-based differential sensing: temporal misalignment, phase ambiguity, and environmental sensitivity. It introduces algorithms and signal processing techniques that align and interpret time-shifted data from multiple ICs, along with a user interface that automatically detects sensors in the field and displays their readings in real time.

- **5x** faster sensory resolution through improved data alignment and robustness.
- **0.79°** median differential phase error under dynamic conditions.
- **8x** higher RFID read throughput from a custom data-collection platform.
