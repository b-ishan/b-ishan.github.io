ZenseTag is a fully passive, miniaturized, flexible sensor interface that reads and communicates analog sensor data directly at radio frequencies. It exploits resonance, where a sensor's terminal impedance is most sensitive to the measured stimulus at its resonant frequency, so the tag can read sensor variation using only energy harvested from wireless signals.

- A **15x10 mm** flexible PCB connects sensors to a printed antenna and passive RFID ICs, with near real-time readout through GUI-enabled software.
- Demonstrated with commercial **force**, **soil-moisture**, and **photodiode** sensors.
- Earned all five ACM artifact evaluation badges: available, functional, reusable, results reproduced, and results replicated.
